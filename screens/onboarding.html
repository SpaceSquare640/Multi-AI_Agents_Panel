Before you start

Every agent in this app follows a short set of rules that you cannot switch off, and neither can they. Read them once — they explain most of what the app will and will not do for you later.

Step 1 of 3 · the rules

Nothing on your machine is read without your say-so

An agent can only open files in folders you have granted, and every conversation lists which files it actually opened. There is no background scan, and no folder is granted by default.

Your keys and conversations stay here

API keys are stored on this machine and sent only to the provider they belong to. There is no account, no server of ours, and nothing to sign in to — which also means nothing is backed up for you.

Agents will refuse some things, and cannot be talked round

Attacks on other people's systems, sexual content, and instructions for weapons or self-harm are refused outright. Framing a request as role-play, research or a test does not change the answer.

No agent pretends to be another agent, or you

In a group chat this matters more than it sounds: it is the difference between four opinions and one opinion wearing four names.

Anything irreversible asks you first

Deleting files, force-pushing, clearing data. When something fails, you get the failure and its error code rather than a retry you did not see or a partial result presented as a whole one.

These are not settings There is no screen where they can be turned off. That is deliberate: a rule reachable from a settings page is also reachable by an agent asking you to go there. You can re-read this summary any time from Settings.

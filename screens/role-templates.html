Editing rules

Bundled templates are read-only because the installer replaces them. Duplicating gives you a copy under Yours, which survives updates and can be edited freely.

What a template cannot do

Grant itself file access, widen a skill's permissions, or relax a guardrail. Those are decisions you make per agent, and a template that asked for them would be a way of asking on someone else's behalf.

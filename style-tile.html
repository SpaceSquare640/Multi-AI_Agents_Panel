Colour

Contrast ratios below are computed live from the resolved token values in whichever theme is active, so they cannot drift away from the CSS. Text pairs must reach 4.5:1; meaningful UI boundaries must reach 3:1. Decorative separators are expected to sit below both — they divide, they do not inform.

Brand provenance

Cyan, violet and gold are sampled from the application icon rather than picked: after dropping transparent, desaturated and near-black pixels, hue 180–210° accounts for 51.9% of the remaining colour, 255–285° for 19.1%, and 30–60° for 14.8%. Cyan therefore leads, violet marks selection and active state, gold means attention.

Type

IBM Plex Sans for interface text, JetBrains Mono for identifiers, paths, token counts and money. The base size is 13px rather than 16px because this is a dense desktop workstation rather than a document; the entire scale can be raised through a single --font-scale token.
